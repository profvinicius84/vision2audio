# Product Context

## Product vision

Vision 2 Audio is an Android accessibility app for visually impaired users that captures a scene on demand, sends the photo and GPS coordinates directly to OpenAI, and speaks a Brazilian Portuguese description of the environment and nearby context.

## Goals

- Help the user understand where they are and what is happening around them.
- Capture scenes quickly from a simple physical trigger.
- Return useful spoken and visible feedback without requiring a backend or login.

## Users or personas

- Visually impaired Android users.
- Science fair demonstrators and reviewers.

## Value proposition

The app reduces the effort needed to understand surroundings by turning a single button press into an immediate, voice-first scene description with GPS context.
