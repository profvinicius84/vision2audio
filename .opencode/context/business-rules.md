# Business Rules Context

## Approved business rules

- Scene capture is initiated only by the approved Bluetooth remote or keyboard trigger.
- The app sends the request directly to OpenAI without a backend.
- No user authentication is required.
- History entries are read-only except for clear-all.
- Responses are spoken automatically and also shown as text.
- Output is always Brazilian Portuguese.
